About & process

A studio built around one idea: show, don't tell.

Most agencies ask you to pay for a promise. Pivotal Nova would rather hand you the thing itself — a working first build — and let it make the argument.

Software for brands of every type

Pivotal Nova is a software development studio. We build websites, web apps and mobile apps — for the barbershop that needs online booking, the café that wants its own ordering app, the growing firm whose bookings have outgrown a spreadsheet, and every brand in between that has been quoted agency prices for template work.

The pattern we kept seeing: owners burned by DIY builders that couldn't grow, or by agencies that charged up front for designs they had to imagine. Both ask the client to carry the risk. So we flipped it. Our first build is free, because the work should be judged as a thing you can open, tap and read — not as a pitch.

A nova is the moment a star pivots and blazes. That's the point we build for: the moment a brand turns.

Everything is designed per brand, from its own world — its tools, its materials, its customers' habits — then engineered properly behind the scenes: dashboards shaped around the business, automations for the repetitive work, integrations with the tools already in use, and the SEO, AEO and GEO groundwork that gets the brand found on Google, in local results and in AI answers.

We are remote-first by design. It keeps overheads low, keeps the process in writing, and means a brand in Manchester, Toronto or Texas gets the same studio, the same standard and the same straight answers.

The process, in depth

Four steps. No surprises.

01

Discover

A short call — half an hour, no jargon, no obligation. We map your brand, your customers, and the one job your website or app has to do: bookings, orders, enquiries, or running the back office.

You get

A clear picture of what we'd build and why — in writing, before anything is built.

02

First build

We design and build a working first version: real pages, your content, your brand, the core flows running. Not a mockup and not a template — the actual thing, on a link you can open on your phone.

You get

A live first version to click through, share with your team, and judge as a customer would. Still £0 / $0 paid.

03

Review & refine

You tell us what to change — words, pages, features, direction. We refine it. And now, with the real thing in front of you, we agree the exact price for the full project. Love it: we proceed. Don't: you walk away, owing nothing.

You get

Your changes made, an exact price with no surprises, and a genuine free choice.

04

Launch & grow

We finish the build, set up hosting on your accounts, hand over your dashboard, and put the visibility groundwork in place — SEO, AEO and GEO — so the launch is a starting line, not the finish.

You get

A live product you own, a dashboard you control, and a studio that stays available as you grow.

Why build-first

Because you should never pay for a promise.

Traditional hiring puts every risk on the client: deposit first, see something months later, pay again to fix what you never approved. Build-first reverses the order — and the risk.

It also makes us better builders. When the first version has to earn your payment, it gets built like it matters.
