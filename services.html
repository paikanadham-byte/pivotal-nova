Straight answers

Asked before every project

How does build-first actually work?+
We start with a short discover call, then build a working first version of your website or app — real pages, your content, your brand. You review it and ask for changes. Only if you want to go ahead do you pay. If it is not for you, you walk away at no cost. The risk sits with us, not you.
What does it cost?+
Every project is priced individually, based on its scope — and you see the exact price after reviewing your first build, before you commit to anything. There are no package prices on this page because a five-page website and a booking platform are not the same job. What we promise is this: you never pay for something you haven't seen working.
What do you need from me to start?+
One short call and the basics of your brand — what you sell, who you sell it to, and anything you already have (a logo, photos, an old site). We handle design, build, hosting setup and the technical side from there.
Can you rebuild the website or app I already have?+
Yes — and it works the same way. We build the new version first, alongside your existing one, so you compare the real thing against what you have now before deciding anything.
Where are you based? What if I'm in another country?+
Pivotal Nova is remote-first and works with brands across the UK, Canada and the US — and worldwide beyond that. Updates land in writing, calls are arranged around your time zone, and you always review real builds, never slide decks.
What happens after launch?+
Your site or app goes live on your own domain and accounts, your dashboard is handed over, and the SEO, AEO and GEO setup is in place. From there we stay available for refinements, new features and growth work — nothing is locked in with us.
